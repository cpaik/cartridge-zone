Make a cart

Write a character, give it meters and win or lose conditions. Give them likes and dislikes, and the rules turn what the player says into meter changes. The model voices the character.

Written as "You are…". This is what the model reads to play the character.
Two or three example lines, one per line. Small models copy these closely.
Words or phrases, comma-separated. Each one the player says raises the meter.
Each one lowers the meter. Kindness, questions and insults are scored for every cart.

The rules decide every change to the meter; the model only writes the character's lines. Custom LoRA adapters are coming next.

Share it

A cart is just this JSON. Copy it to share; paste someone else's to import.

To run the real version: save the file, run python3 -m http.server 8000 in its folder, then open localhost:8000/cartridge-zone.html in Chrome, Edge, or Safari 26+.
